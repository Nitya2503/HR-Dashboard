# HR-Dashboard

Short Description

A comprehensive HR analytics dashboard that visualizes employee demographics, attrition patterns, job satisfaction, and workforce distribution to support data-driven HR decisions.

Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to understand why employees leave, which job roles or salary bands face higher attrition, and how demographic factors influence workforce stability. This lack of clarity affects workforce planning and increases hiring costs.

Goal of the Dashboard

To monitor workforce composition and analyze attrition trends across education level, age groups, job roles, salary ranges, and years of experience—helping HR teams identify issues early and improve retention strategies.

Business Impact

- Reduced Attrition Costs: Highlights high-risk groups to focus retention efforts.
- Smarter Workforce Planning: Identifies job roles with talent shortages.
- Improved Employee Experience: Reveals satisfaction patterns to guide HR interventions.
- Better Compensation Strategy: Shows salary ranges with the highest attrition.
